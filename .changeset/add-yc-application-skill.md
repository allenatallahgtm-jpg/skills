Add the `yc-application` skill (productivity bucket, model-invoked). It drafts a ready-to-submit Y Combinator application from the founder's real facts: a batched intake that builds a fact sheet, every form section (company, founders, progress, idea, why now, competitors, business model, risks, equity, curious), a 1-minute founder video script, and a self-review checklist before hand-over.

Its defining rule is that it never invents traction, metrics, customers or investors: anything the founder hasn't supplied becomes a `[FOUNDER: ...]` placeholder, listed back as a question. Invented numbers live only in its two clearly labelled fictional example applications (B2B SaaS and healthcare), which it reads for shape. Ships with domain risk guidance (healthcare HIPAA/BAA, clinical liability, sales cycle, who pays; fintech; marketplaces; hardware; and more), a docs page, and an `ask-matt` routing entry.
