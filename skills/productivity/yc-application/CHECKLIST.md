# Self-review checklist

Run every item against the finished file. Fix each failure in the draft; a failure that only the founder can fix becomes a placeholder in the open-placeholders list.

## Truth

- [ ] Every number, customer, investor, quote and founder achievement traces to a row in the fact sheet, and every fact-sheet row came from the founder, arithmetic shown with its inputs, or a cited public source.
- [ ] Nothing from the fictional examples appears in the draft: no Tallyport, no Inflow Health, none of their figures or phrasing presented as the founder's.
- [ ] Every gap is a `[FOUNDER: ...]` placeholder specific enough to answer in one line, and every placeholder appears in the open-placeholders list at the end of the file.
- [ ] Each number appears identically everywhere it is used: sections, video script and fact sheet agree.

## Strength

- [ ] The one-liner is 50 characters or fewer (counted, with the count shown), and says what the product does and for whom.
- [ ] Every answer's first sentence answers the question.
- [ ] Traction is stated as numbers with time frames, not adjectives.
- [ ] Founder-market fit is explicit: why these founders, for this problem.
- [ ] "Who writes code" names people and what they built.
- [ ] Why now names a specific change, not a general trend.
- [ ] Competitors are named, the status quo is included, and the insight is a claim someone could disagree with.
- [ ] The business model has a price, a unit, and market-size math with its inputs.
- [ ] Risks cover the domain's expected risks from [DOMAINS.md](DOMAINS.md), each with a real mitigation or a placeholder.
- [ ] Equity percentages sum to 100%.

## Readability

- [ ] Plain language: no "revolutionary", "disrupt", "leverage", "seamless", "synergy", "AI-powered platform", or a vision statement in place of a fact.
- [ ] No answer carries a sentence that adds no fact. Each fits the form's limits where the founder supplied them.
- [ ] The video script is 150 spoken words or fewer, every founder speaks, and it includes the single best number.
